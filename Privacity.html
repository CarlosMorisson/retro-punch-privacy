Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This document explains how Retro Punch handles information when you play on Meta Quest devices.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Retro Punch, developed and published by CARLOS MORISSON, handles user information when playing the game on Meta Quest devices.

Retro Punch is designed to operate without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Retro Punch does not directly collect, sell, or share personally identifiable information.

The game may process the following information locally on your Meta Quest device:

  • Hand Tracking Data: Hand movements and gestures are processed in real time for gameplay.
  • Controller Input: Controller movements and button interactions support gameplay.
  • Game Settings: Audio preferences, difficulty settings and configurations may be stored locally.
  • Gameplay Progress: Scores and progress may be stored locally on your device.

We do not operate our own servers to collect or store player information. Meta may independently process information according to its own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed locally is used exclusively to:

  • Provide the core gameplay experience.
  • Enable hand tracking and controller interactions.
  • Save local game settings and progress.
  • Improve game functionality and performance.

4. Data Sharing

Retro Punch does not share personal information with third parties because we do not directly collect or maintain personal user databases.

Any information independently processed by Meta is subject to Meta's own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Retro Punch does not maintain external databases containing personal user information.

Game settings and progress stored locally remain on the user's device. Users may remove locally stored application data through their Meta Quest device settings or by uninstalling the application.

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us using the email address below.

6. Children's Privacy

Retro Punch does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not require users to submit personal information or create an account.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
